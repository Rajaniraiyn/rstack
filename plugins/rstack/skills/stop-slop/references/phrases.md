# Phrases to cut or replace

## AI vocabulary

delve, additionally, crucial, enduring, enhance, fostering, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape (abstract), pivotal, showcase, tapestry (abstract), testament, underscore, vibrant, leverage (as verb), utilize, facilitate, empower, streamline, robust (when it means nothing specific), seamless, cutting-edge, paradigm shift, game changer, elevate, embark, supercharge, harness (as metaphor), ever-evolving.

Replace with plain words: "use", "help", "look", "build", "support", "solid", "new", "big change", or the specific fact. If the word carries no meaning beyond impressiveness, delete it.

## Fancy ways to say "is"

serves as, stands as, boasts, features, marks, represents. Just say "is" or "has".

## Throat-clearing openers

Here's the thing, Here's what I mean, Let me be clear, I'll be honest, The uncomfortable truth is, The reality is, The truth is, What most people get wrong, Here's what nobody tells you, The part everyone misses, Let's dive in, Let's unpack, Let's break this down, In this article, In this section, Today we're going to. Cut them and state the point.

## Filler phrases

In order to, Due to the fact that, It is important to note that, It's worth noting, It bears mentioning, Importantly, Interestingly, Notably, At the end of the day, When it comes to, In the world of, In the age of, In today's world, Going forward, In terms of, With regard to, As we've seen, As discussed above.

## Hedges

Could potentially possibly, Arguably, It might be the case that, Generally speaking, To some extent, In many ways. Keep "I think" or "maybe" when they carry real uncertainty or the writer's spoken rhythm.

## Vague attributions

Experts agree, Experts believe, Industry reports suggest, Studies show, Researchers have shown, Many argue, Widely regarded as, Several publications have cited, Observers have noted. Name the source or cut the claim. If you can't name the expert, you don't have a source.

## Empty adverbs

just, literally, honestly, simply, actually, truly, fundamentally, importantly, crucially, inherently, inevitably, notably, interestingly, significantly, dramatically, remarkably, undeniably, clearly (when the claim isn't visibly clear), obviously. Cut them when they add nothing. Keep them when they carry emphasis, uncertainty, contrast, or the writer's natural voice.

## Superficial -ing analyses

highlighting, underscoring, reflecting, showcasing, contributing to, fostering. These tack fake significance onto a fact. Replace with the actual consequence: "The launch adds file search, so users can find old drafts without leaving the editor."

## Business buzzwords

ecosystem (abstract), landscape, synergy, circle back, move the needle, low-hanging fruit, best-in-class, world-class, mission-critical, bandwidth (as capacity), touch base, drill down, key takeaway, actionable, pivot, holistic, granular, next-generation, industry-leading. Cut them or name the actual thing.

## Chatbot phrases

I hope this helps, Let me know if you have any questions, Feel free to reach out, Don't hesitate to ask, Of course, Certainly (as a standalone reply), Absolutely (as a standalone reply), Happy to help, Glad to help, No problem, Let me know if you need anything else, I'm here if you need anything. Remove. Respond directly.

## Importance puffery

Stands as a testament to, Marks a pivotal moment, Plays a vital role, Solidifies its position, Underscores its significance, This changes everything, This is huge. State the fact and let the reader judge whether it matters: "The launch is the company's first paid product."

## Fake-strong verbs

grants the ability to, serves to, acts as, functions as, provides the capability to. Replace with "lets", "can", "is", "does".

## Filler transitions

Moving on, Next up, Let's talk about, It's also worth mentioning, On a related note, That said (when it links nothing), More importantly. Cut them.

## Domain terms are fine

Precise field terminology is not slop. "Weighted interval score" is precise language. "Idempotent" is precise language. The problem is buzzwords and AI tells leaking into text where they add nothing.
