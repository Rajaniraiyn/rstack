# Document and report slop

Fix AI patterns in documents and reports without changing what they say.

## Structure

- A summary or introduction that restates later sections word for word. Keep one sentence of what the document is, then deliver.
- "As we saw above", "as discussed earlier", "recall that". Cut the callbacks.
- A conclusion that repeats the body. End on the decision, the numbers, or the next action.
- Signposted structure: "In this section, we'll explore", "In conclusion", "Ultimately". Cut.
- Every section the same shape: point, example, recap. Vary the shape with the job.
- A listicle in prose clothing: "The first wall... The second wall... The third wall...". Write paragraphs.

## Formatting

- Bold-first bullets: every list item opening with a bolded keyword. Remove the bold leads.
- Emoji in headings. Remove.
- Headers over two-sentence sections. Merge or cut.
- Bullets where two sentences of prose would read better. Write prose.
- Em dashes. Replace with periods or commas.
- Curly quotes and unicode arrows. Use straight quotes and plain text.

## Language

- Filler transitions: "it's worth noting", "importantly", "notably", "interestingly", "it bears mentioning". Cut.
- Vague declaratives: "the implications are significant". Name the specific implication.
- Passive voice with a known actor. Name the actor.
- Hedges: "could potentially possibly be argued that it might". Use "may".
- Long sentences past the point of parsing. Split them.
- Superficial "-ing" analyses: "highlighting", "underscoring", "showcasing". Replace with the real consequence.
- Invented claims, stats, or comparisons. Never fabricate. Use a labeled placeholder or cut.
