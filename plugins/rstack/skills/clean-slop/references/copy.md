# Copy and website content slop

Fix AI patterns in copy and web content without changing the message.

## Claims

- Vague claims without numbers or sources: "trusted by thousands", "saves you hours". Use a real count or cut it.
- Fabricated metrics, logos, testimonials, and case-study counts. Never invent. Use a labeled placeholder ("metric to confirm") or a different layout.
- Vague attribution: "experts agree", "industry reports suggest". Name the source or cut the claim.

## Language

- Buzzwords: leverage, seamless, world-class, best-in-class, cutting-edge, game-changing, elevate, empower, supercharge. Say what the product does.
- Fake-strong verbs: "serves as", "stands as", "boasts". Say "is" or "has".
- Patronizing analogies: "think of it as", "it's like a...". Say the thing.
- Futurism: "imagine a world where". Cut.
- Abstract metaphor nouns: ecosystem, landscape, tapestry, realm, beacon, north star, flywheel. Use the plain concrete word.
- Superficial "-ing" analyses: "highlighting", "underscoring", "showcasing". Replace with the real consequence.

## Rhythm

- Stacked tricolons and punchy fragments for manufactured emphasis. Use full sentences.
- Robotic symmetry: every paragraph the same shape. Vary sentence length and structure.
- Copy that could sit unchanged on any competitor's site. That is filler. Make it specific to this product, with facts, numbers, or mechanisms.

## Proof

- Keep behavior and meaning unchanged when editing. Match the brand's existing tone.
- Never invent quotes from users. If the brief has no testimonial, cut the section or leave a labeled placeholder.
